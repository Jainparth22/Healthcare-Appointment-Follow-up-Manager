Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ today }}

{{ app_name }} ("the App", "we") provides appointment booking, AI-assisted visit summaries, and medication reminders for clinic patients and doctors. This policy explains what we collect and how it is used.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email, phone (optional), and a securely hashed password.
  • Appointment data: chosen slots, symptoms you submit, doctor notes, prescriptions, and reminder schedules.
  • AI summaries: symptoms and doctor notes are sent to a third-party LLM provider (Google Gemini) to generate pre-visit and post-visit summaries. The prompt and output are stored for auditability.
  • Google Calendar: if you connect your calendar, we store an OAuth refresh token encrypted at rest solely to create/update/delete event entries for your appointments. Disconnecting removes access.
  • Email: confirmations, cancellations, reschedules, visit summaries, and reminders are delivered to the email address on your account.

2. How we use data

  • Operate the booking, reminder, and notification features.
  • Generate the AI summaries requested by patients and doctors.
  • Sync appointments to calendars you explicitly connect.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Calendar at any time from your dashboard.
  • Cancel appointments from the patient portal.
  • Contact the clinic administrator to request deletion of your account and associated records.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; calendar tokens are encrypted (Fernet); all traffic is served over HTTPS. Access is role-based (patient / doctor / admin).
